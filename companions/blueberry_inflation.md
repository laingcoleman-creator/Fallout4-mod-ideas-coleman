🫐 Blueberry Inflation Mod (Violet Beauregarde Companion)
✔ Feasible?
Surprisingly yes — but only with limits.

🔧 What’s easy:
A companion who emits “blue juice” consumables

A quest triggered by Pip‑Boy radio

A perk reward

A gum item that applies a “blueberry morph”

AP‑based rolling attacks (Pain Train synergy)

🔥 What’s hard:
Body inflation morphs

Blue spreading across the skin

Leaving puddles or trails

Physics for a spherical body

Custom animations for rolling

💰️ Money required?
Only if you want custom morphs and animations.

🧠 Verdict:
A stylized, lore‑friendly version is doable. A full Wonka‑accurate transformation system is extremely advanced.
